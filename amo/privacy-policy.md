Zebu's privacy policy applies to this extension: https://zebu.work/privacy

The extension talks only to the Zebu workspace you connect it to (your-workspace.zebu.work): it signs you in there, starts and stops your timers, and lists your projects and entries. It contacts no other service, has no analytics and shows no ads. The workspace address and the sign-in token are kept in the browser's extension storage and removed when you disconnect.
